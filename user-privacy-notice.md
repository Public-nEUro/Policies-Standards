## What data do we collect?

We collect the following data:

- Personal identification information, including name, email address, phone number and similar contact details.
- Work or institutional address.

## How do we collect your data?

You directly provide all of the data we collect. We collect and process data when you:

- set up a service contract with us as a dataset author;
- have your information provided by a dataset author as part of a private hosting service;
- register online as a service user.

## How will we use your data?

PublicnEUro collects your data so that we can process your order, share your data or provide any other service you have purchased, or manage your account and give you access to data.

PublicnEUro may send user information related to data access to the data authors, who are the data controllers, and also to supervisory authorities for audit purposes.

## How do we store your data?

PublicnEUro securely stores your data on the Neurobiology Research Unit servers.

We will keep your personal data information indefinitely, i.e. without a planned end date, because audits of who accesses research datasets are mandatory.

## What are your data protection rights?

We would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

_The right to access_: You have the right to request copies of your personal data from PublicnEUro. We may charge you a small fee for this service if requested too often.

_The right to rectification_: You have the right to request that PublicnEUro correct any information you believe is inaccurate. You also have the right to request PublicnEUro to complete the information you believe is incomplete.

_The right to erasure_: You have the right to request that PublicnEUro erases your personal data, under certain conditions, for instance, if you have never downloaded datasets.

_The right to restrict processing_: You have the right to request that PublicnEUro restricts the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.

_The right to object to processing_: You have the right to object to PublicnEUro's processing of your personal data, under certain conditions, for instance, if as a dataset author you have changed institution and are no longer the point of contact.

_The right to data portability_: You have the right to request that PublicnEUro transfers the data that we have collected to another organisation, or directly to you, under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

## Privacy policy for our website

In keeping with the spirit of Open Science, the PublicnEUro website is fully open, and you can find the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/Public-nEUro/Public-nEUro.github.io). The hosting is also done via GitHub, and this means your data as a website visitor are processed according to the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement#how-github-uses-your-information).

## Privacy policies of other websites

The PublicnEUro website contains links to other websites. Our privacy policy applies only to our website, so if you click on a link to another website, you should read their privacy policy.

## Changes to our privacy policy

PublicnEUro keeps its privacy policy under regular review and places any updates on this web page. This privacy policy was last updated on 18 June 2024.
